# Material Icon Theme assets

readme.svg, markdown.svg, and document.svg come from [vscode-material-icon-theme](https://github.com/material-extensions/vscode-material-icon-theme) at commit 9d513c781fb5bd0619ca21bd519fd1df5f19f84a. The adjacent PNGs are 64 × 64 renders of those SVGs. KDE copies the same PNGs into its QML resources, while macOS bundles them in the app and GNOME embeds them in the executable.
